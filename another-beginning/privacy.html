适用范围
本政策适用于包名 com.lvjing.jiaru 的 Google Play 安卓版《假如换个开局》。本游戏面向13岁及以上的青少年和成人，不面向13岁以下儿童。微信和抖音版本不在本政策范围内。

游戏记录
当前安卓版本不要求创建游戏账号。游戏进度、人物经历、个人输入、设置和档案保存在设备内。游戏不会主动将完整存档或个人输入发送到 TrvelStudio 的游戏后台，也不会把这些内容作为广告请求参数。安卓版本支持你主动生成一张人生卡片，通过 X（Twitter）、Facebook、Instagram 或系统分享面板交给你选择的应用。卡片包含模拟人生摘要和商店链接，不包含完整存档、购买令牌或个人感想；在目标应用中确认发送前，不会自动公开。接收方的数据处理适用其隐私政策。安卓版本不会把这张卡片上传到我们的游戏后台；微信版的联网分享不属于本政策范围。人物的健康、财富等数值属于模拟游戏数据，不是对玩家现实状况的测量。

广告服务
游戏使用 Google AdMob、Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）和 Unity Ads。广告服务可能处理 IP 推断的大致位置、用户标识符、设备或其他标识符、广告互动及诊断信息，用于广告投放与衡量、服务运行和防范欺诈。具体方式见下方合作方政策。没有显示广告不代表广告服务没有处理数据。
游戏不会向你索取银行卡资料、支付账号密码、通讯录或精确定位。Unity 的通用 SDK 披露还包含购买历史等类别；具体数据处理以相应服务的说明和你所使用版本的功能为准。内部技术预览版可能关闭广告及购买入口。

内购与恢复购买
支持购买的版本由 Google Play 处理一次性去插屏商品。为核验和恢复已购买权益，应用向本游戏位于新加坡的后台传输购买令牌、商品标识和应用包名，后台向 Google 核验并确认订单。我们不接收银行卡资料或完整游戏存档，不在后台持久保存原始购买令牌；本机缓存已验证权益和验证时间，离线有效期最长7天，联网后会重新核验。服务以经过摘要处理的网络标识短期记录请求次数以防滥用，计数最多保留约48小时。Google 按其政策保存交易资料。清除本地数据不会取消 Google Play 交易；可使用同一个 Google Play 账号恢复有效购买。

你的选择
在适用地区，广告隐私提示由 UMP 提供。系统要求提供隐私选择入口时，可以在游戏设置中重新打开“广告隐私选择”。可请求广告不等于已同意个性化广告。你也可以使用 Android 的广告标识符设置。没有可用广告时，不会阻止你开始下一段人生。

保存、删除与联系
清除应用数据或卸载应用可以删除该安装实例的本地记录，同时会失去进度。设备备份及恢复还取决于系统；当前应用已关闭 Android 应用自动备份。清除本地数据不等于删除广告服务已持有的数据。关于第三方数据的访问、删除、保留期限或其他隐私权利，请使用其政策中的渠道；也可以通过上方邮箱联系 TrvelStudio 寻求协助。请勿发送密码或支付凭证。

第三方与跨境处理
Google 与 Unity 可能在你所在地区以外处理广告数据，适用其政策中的安排。两家官方披露均说明传输数据采用加密。我们不替第三方承诺统一的保留期限。

Another Beginning — Google Play Android Privacy Policy
Updated: November 6, 2026

Operator: TrvelStudio
Contact: flyyamarger@gmail.com

Scope
This policy covers the Google Play Android edition of Another Beginning (假如换个开局), package com.lvjing.jiaru. The game is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, and is not directed at children under 13. It does not describe the separate WeChat or Douyin editions.

Game records
The current Android edition does not require an account. Progress, character histories, personal notes, preferences and archives are stored on your device. The game does not proactively upload full saves or notes to TrvelStudio's backend or include them as advertising request parameters. Android lets you generate a story-card image and choose X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram or the Android share sheet. The card contains a simulated-life summary and store link, not the full save, purchase token or personal notes. Nothing is automatically published: you choose a receiving app and confirm there. Its privacy policy governs what you send. Android does not upload these cards to our game server; the separate WeChat online-sharing feature is outside this policy. Character health and financial values are fictional simulation data, not measurements of the player.

Advertising
We integrate Google AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Unity Ads. Advertising services may process approximate location derived from IP addresses, device or other identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, operation and fraud prevention. No displayed ads does not necessarily mean no data processing. See the providers' policies below.
We do not request card details, payment account passwords, contacts or precise location. Unity's general SDK disclosures also include purchase history. Processing depends on the services and features enabled in your version. Internal technical previews may disable advertising and purchases.

Purchases and restoration
Versions supporting purchases use Google Play for the one-time removal of interstitial ads. To verify and restore purchases, the app sends the purchase token, product ID and package name to our game backend in Singapore, which verifies and acknowledges the purchase with Google. We do not receive card details or full game saves and do not persist raw purchase tokens. The device caches verified entitlement and its verification time for up to seven days offline, with re-verification when connected. Hashed network identifiers are used for short-term request limiting for up to approximately 48 hours. Google retains transaction information under its own policies. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play transaction; valid purchases can be restored using the same Google Play account.

Choices
UMP supplies advertising privacy prompts where applicable. When required, you can reopen advertising privacy choices from game settings. Eligibility to request ads is not treated as consent to personalized advertising. You can also use Android's advertising identifier controls. Unavailable ads do not prevent starting another life.

Storage and deletion
Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this installation's local records and loses local progress. Device backup and restoration also depend on the system; this app disables Android app auto-backup. Deleting local records does not delete data already held by advertising providers. Use their policy channels for access, deletion, retention information or other privacy rights, or contact TrvelStudio for assistance. Do not email passwords or payment credentials.

Providers and international processing
Google and Unity may process advertising data outside your region under arrangements described in their policies. Both providers disclose encryption in transit. We do not promise a uniform retention period on their behalf.
