Arrow Garden
Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers the Google Play Android edition of Arrow Garden, package com.lvjing.arrowgarden, operated by Wenze Ma under the developer name TrvelStudio. For privacy and purchase support, contact flyyamarger@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

The game stores progress, scores, collected patterns and keepsakes, achievements, earned items, language and sound preferences on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove this data. Any Android backup enabled on your device is handled by its provider. Restoring purchases is separate from restoring game progress.

Ads and privacy choices

The Android build includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform, Unity Ads, and mediation adapters for AppLovin and Liftoff/Vungle. Which providers serve ads depends on the build, available inventory, configuration, your region and applicable consent. An included adapter does not mean that provider will serve an ad.

Ad services and their partners may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Personalization depends on your choices and applicable requirements. Limited or non-personalized ads may still involve data processing. Test ads may be used during testing.

Where required, the game requests privacy choices before eligible ad requests. Use the game's Privacy Options when available to review your choices, and Android settings to manage advertising identifiers. Rewarded videos are optional. The displayed reward is granted after the completion callback. The remove-interstitials product applies to automatic interstitial ads, while optional rewarded videos remain available.

Purchases and recovery

Google Play processes payments and displays the price and payment confirmation. We do not receive your payment-card details or Google password. A supporting service creates a random purchase-account identifier and recovery key. The device retains the recovery key and the service stores its hash. Keep this key private.

To deliver or restore items and verify entitlements, the game sends purchase tokens, product identifiers and the random account identifier over HTTPS to our service. The service processes purchase quantities and status, item balances and uses, refund and revocation records, and technical timestamps. Google Play APIs verify orders and refund status. A pending or cancelled payment does not grant items. Refunds or revocations can withdraw associated benefits after verification.

Purchase records are sensitive. They are retained to provide purchased benefits and recovery, resolve delivery problems, prevent fraud and handle refund and legal obligations. Access is restricted. HTTPS protects transmission. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Service providers

Google provides billing, advertising and consent messaging. Configured advertising partners provide ads and measurement under their own privacy policies. Supporting services use cloud infrastructure, including Tencent Cloud. Service providers may process data outside your country. Technical access logs can contain IP addresses, request paths, timestamps and response status. Purchase-verification records are used for fulfilment and fraud prevention.

Provider policies include Google, Unity, AppLovin and Liftoff.

Retention and requests

Local progress remains until you clear it or uninstall the app, subject to device backup settings. Service records are kept while needed for purchase delivery, recovery, disputes, fraud prevention and applicable obligations. Support correspondence is kept for resolving the request and related obligations. Providers determine retention for data they control.

Send access, correction or deletion requests to flyyamarger@gmail.com. Include the game name and a description of your request. We may ask for limited information to verify the request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, full card details or identity documents. Removing purchase-account records can affect item recovery, and some records may need to be retained for disputes or legal obligations. A deletion request does not cancel a purchase or request a refund. For information controlled by Google or an advertising provider, use that provider's privacy controls or request channels.

Audience and updates

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child's information was improperly processed. We update this policy when the game's data practices change and seek additional consent when required.
